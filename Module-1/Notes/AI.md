What is Artificial Intelligence?
    Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, decision-making, speech recognition, and understanding natural language.

Goals of AI
  Automate repetitive tasks.
  Improve decision-making.
  Solve complex problems efficiently.
  Mimic human intelligence.
  Enhance productivity and accuracy.

Types of AI
  1. Narrow AI (Weak AI)
    Designed for a specific task.
    Examples:
    Siri
    Google Assistant
    Face Unlock
    ChatGPT
  2. General AI (Strong AI)
    Can perform any intellectual task like a human.
    Still under research.
  3. Super AI
    Intelligence beyond human capabilities.
    A future concept.

Advantages of AI
  Reduces human effort.
  Works 24/7.
  High accuracy.
  Faster decision-making.
  Automates repetitive work.
  Disadvantages of AI
  High development cost.
  Requires large amounts of data.
  Can replace certain jobs.
  Lacks human emotions and creativity.

Conclusion
  Artificial Intelligence is transforming industries by making systems smarter and more efficient. It has applications in healthcare, education, finance, transportation, entertainment, and many other fields.
