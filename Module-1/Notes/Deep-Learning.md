# 🧠 Deep Learning (DL)

## What is Deep Learning?

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers to learn from large amounts of data. It is designed to solve complex problems such as image recognition, speech recognition, and language translation.

---

## Features of Deep Learning

- Uses Artificial Neural Networks (ANN).
- Learns automatically from data.
- Requires a large amount of data.
- Delivers high accuracy for complex tasks.

---

## Applications of Deep Learning

- Image Recognition
- Face Recognition
- Speech Recognition
- Self-driving Cars
- Medical Diagnosis
- Virtual Assistants

---

## Advantages

- High accuracy.
- Handles large datasets.
- Automatically extracts features.
- Solves complex real-world problems.

---

## Disadvantages

- Requires powerful computers.
- Needs a large amount of training data.
- Training takes more time.
- Difficult to understand the model.

---

## Difference Between AI, ML, and DL

| AI | ML | DL |
|----|----|----|
| Makes machines intelligent | Learns from data | Uses neural networks |
| Broadest field | Subset of AI | Subset of ML |
| Rule-based or learning | Data-driven | Deep neural networks |

---

## Conclusion

Deep Learning is an advanced technology that powers modern AI applications such as voice assistants, autonomous vehicles, medical imaging, and intelligent recommendation systems.
